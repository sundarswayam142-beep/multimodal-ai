WEEK 3 NOTES: STEPPING INTO DEEP LEARNING ARCHITECTURES AND CNNS
================================================================
Shifted away from simple statistics into multi-layered neural models this week.

1. FOUNDATIONAL REASONING NETWORKS
   - Layer Connections: Got an intuitive grasp of matrix multiplications inside a neural network, tracking how raw feature shapes scale as they move forward.
   - Adjusting Weights: Realized that training models is essentially a game of trial and error where gradients nudge internal parameters closer to target predictions.

2. CONVOLUTIONAL LAYERS FOR IMAGE PROCESSING
   - Feature Maps: Explored how sliding convolutional filters extract small spatial textures like lines, edges, and metallic micro-fractures directly from image feeds.
   - Pooling Layers: Figured out that Max Pooling simply picks out the most intense feature markers from a region, downsizing parameters so my machine doesn't choke.
   - Flattening & Output: Unrolled internal tensor arrays into linear shapes to run classification probabilities at the final bottleneck layer.
